About me

Engineering is the craft. Learning is the constant.

A little more context behind the person, the work and the direction I'm taking.

I'm Safiullah Korai, a Software Engineer, Flutter Developer and Technical Writer. I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept to something people can actually use.

My current engineering focus is Flutter and practical software development. Alongside that, I'm deliberately building the foundations for my next direction: AI Engineering.

That means going deeper into Python, machine learning, deep learning, data, APIs and the systems around intelligent applications — while keeping software engineering at the center.

I want to build software that is useful, explainable and worth maintaining.

Writing is part of that process. When I learn something difficult, I like breaking it down, documenting it and sharing the parts that might help another developer.
